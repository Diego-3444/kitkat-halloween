**Team:** Diego Esquivel Martínez · Adiel Santiago Rovira Ochoa · Oziel Alejandro Hernández Serrato
**Claim:** Kit Kat is the best Halloween candy.

### DIEGO — Slide 1 (≈20 sec)

> Good morning. We are Diego, Adiel, and Oziel. We tested three candies — Kit Kat, Reese's Cups,
> and Sour Patch Kids — on flavor, texture, appearance, and Halloween appeal.
> Our claim is simple: **Kit Kat is the best Halloween candy.**

*Say the claim slowly. It's the one sentence the rubric grades hardest.*

### DIEGO — Slide 2 (≈20 sec)

> Here's what we found. Reese's has a strong, salty peanut butter flavor, but it's soft all the way
> through — one texture only — and it melts in a warm candy bag. Sour Patch Kids are fun and colorful,
> but they're sticky, and after three of them your mouth is done.
>
> Kit Kat was the only candy that scored high in **all four** categories. Here's our first reason why.

*Point at the table. Do NOT read it out loud — the class reads faster than you talk.*

### DIEGO — Slide 3 (≈20 sec) → hand off

> Reason one: **texture.** Every other candy in the top ten gives you one texture — soft, chewy, or hard.
> Kit Kat gives you two at the same time: a crisp wafer snap *and* smooth chocolate.
> According to Instacart's 2026 report, chocolate owns the first six spots of the top ten — and Kit Kat
> is the **only crispy wafer bar** in that group. It has no competition on texture.
>
> That's why you can eat four Kit Kats and still want more. Try that with Sour Patch Kids.
> **Adiel will take it from here.**

---

### ADIEL — Slide 4 (≈20 sec) — THE SNAP

> Thank you, Diego. Reason two: **it's built to be shared.** Kit Kat is the only candy engineered to be
> divided — four fingers, no knife, no mess. One bar feeds four trick-or-treaters. You cannot split a
> Reese's Cup evenly.
>
> Halloween is a social holiday, and Hershey knows it: their 2026 Halloween line sells Kit Kat in
> "breakaway shapes." **The brand markets breaking and sharing as the whole point.**

*Press ENTER to snap the bar right as you say "engineered to be divided." If you brought a real Kit Kat, break it here.*

### ADIEL — Slide 5 (≈20 sec)

> Reason three: **Kit Kat owns Halloween.** For Halloween 2026, Hershey released "Kit Kat Count and
> Witch" — the vampire Count came back, and this year he's joined by a brand-new Witch. They also
> launched "Kit Kat Ghost Toast," a limited cinnamon-toast flavor, in the Halloween All-Stars box.
>
> No other candy gets costumes. Reese's changes *shape*; Kit Kat changes *character*.

### ADIEL — Slide 6 (≈25 sec) — REQUIRED ARTICLE EVIDENCE → hand off

> Now our article evidence. We read "Reese's Peanut Butter Cups Named America's Favorite Halloween
> Candy Again in 2026," published by AOL on September thirtieth, reporting Instacart's national data.
>
> The detail we chose: **chocolate claims the first six spots in the top ten, and Kit Kat is ranked sixth.**
>
> In our own words: sour and chewy candies are trending right now — but Americans *still* put chocolate
> in every single one of the top six spots. Kit Kat is inside that locked chocolate group. Out of
> hundreds of candies sold in this country, Kit Kat finishes ahead of **every** sour, gummy, and fruity
> candy there is. **Oziel will answer the objection.**

*SLOW DOWN. This slide is 5 of the 20 points. Make sure the teacher hears the title, the date, and sees the link.*

---

### OZIEL — Slide 7 (≈35 sec) — THE COUNTERARGUMENT

> Thank you, Adiel. Someone is going to push back, so let's say it ourselves: *"Kit Kat is only number
> six, and it dropped from number four last year. That means it's losing."*
>
> Three answers.
> **One:** number six out of hundreds of candies is elite. Kit Kat still beats Twix, Skittles, Sour Patch
> Kids, and every non-chocolate candy on that list.
> **Two:** look at the method. That ranking comes from Instacart *grocery orders* placed in October 2025.
> It measures what **adults bulk-buy online** — not what **kids actually want in the bag.**
> **Three:** two spots inside a crowded top ten is noise, not a trend. And Kit Kat is the only candy in
> the top ten that got a brand-new Halloween character this year. That's a brand *growing*, not shrinking.

*Press ENTER to flip each card as you hit each answer. Answer TWO is the strongest — attacking the method is what separates you from every other group.*

### OZIEL — Slide 8 (≈20 sec) — THE CLOSE

> So: best texture, best to share, best Halloween identity.
> **Reese's wins the grocery list. Kit Kat wins Halloween night.**
> Give Kit Kat the Crown. Thank you — what questions do you have?

*Press ENTER for confetti. Then STOP talking and let them ask.*

## Likely audience questions + prepared answers

**"What about peanut or nut allergies — isn't that your real argument?"**
> Good question. We deliberately didn't build our case on allergies, because Kit Kat is produced on
> shared equipment, so we can't promise it's allergy-safe. Our argument is about texture, sharing, and
> Halloween identity — things we can actually prove.

**"Snickers / M&M's outranks Kit Kat in other surveys. Doesn't that beat your point?"**
> It does show rankings disagree — CandyStore.com's 2026 survey of 3,200-plus customers put M&M's
> first nationally. That's exactly why we didn't argue "Kit Kat is #1 in sales." We argued it's the best
> *Halloween* candy: unique texture, built to share, and the only one with its own Halloween characters.
> No survey ranks those.

**"Isn't 'it breaks into four pieces' a weak reason?"**
> It's the only *functional* advantage any candy on this list has. Halloween is about trading and sharing
> candy. A bar designed to be split is designed for this holiday specifically.

**"Kit Kat is just wafer and chocolate — isn't it boring?"**
> Simple isn't boring, it's why it doesn't burn you out. Intense candies like Sour Patch Kids are great
> for two pieces. Kit Kat is the one still in your bag on November 3rd that you *want* to eat.

*Oziel takes the question, but any of you can add on. Answering together looks like a team.*
