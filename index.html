Step 3 — Counterargument

THEY SAY… WE ANSWER

The strongest objection "Kit Kat is only #6, and it dropped from #4 last year. That means it's losing."
01
The ranking

"Sixth place isn't impressive. Five candies beat it."

Our answer

#6 out of hundreds of candies is elite. Kit Kat still beats Twix, Skittles, Sour Patch Kids, and every non-chocolate candy on the list.

02
The data

"The numbers say Americans prefer other candy."

Our answer — strongest

Look at the method. That ranking comes from Instacart grocery orders placed in October 2025. It measures what adults bulk-buy online — not what kids actually want in the bag.

03
The trend

"Dropping two spots means the brand is shrinking."

Our answer

Two spots in a crowded top 10 is noise, not a trend. And Kit Kat is the only top-10 candy that got a brand-new Halloween character in 2026. That's a brand growing.
